How to play

The idea

Everyone hides a cup of dice. Players take turns claiming how many of a face are on the whole table — bluffing freely — until someone calls the last claim a lie. Lose a die when you are wrong. Last player with dice wins.

Order of play

  1. Everyone rolls and peeks at their own dice.
  2. The starting player opens with a bid, like “three 4s”.
  3. Going clockwise, each player either raises the bid or calls liar.
  4. On a call, everyone lifts their cups and counts that face.
  5. Whoever was wrong loses a die. Everyone re-rolls and the next round begins.

Bidding

A bid is a quantity plus a face, and it covers every die on the table, not just your own. Each bid must beat the one before it:

  • Raise the quantity — three 4s → four 4s (any face is fine once the quantity goes up).
  • Or keep the quantity and raise the face — three 4s → three 5s.

Three 4s → three 2s is not a raise. The face went down.

Ones are wild

A 1 counts as whatever face is being bid. If the bid is “four 3s”, every 3 and every 1 on the table counts toward it. Because of that, nobody bids on 1s directly in most groups.

Calling liar

Instead of raising, challenge the bid. Cups come up and you count the face:

  • Fewer than the bid → the bidder was lying and loses a die.
  • That many or more → the bid was good and the challenger loses a die.

Whoever lost the die presses − here to drop it. At zero dice you are out; the last player standing wins.

Common house rules

  • Spot on: call the bid exactly right and the bidder loses a die instead of you — or everyone else does.
  • Palifico: when a player is down to one die, 1s stop being wild for that round.
  • Some tables play with no wilds at all. Agree before you start.
